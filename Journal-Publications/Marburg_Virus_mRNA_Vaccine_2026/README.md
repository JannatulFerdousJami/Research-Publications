## Research Overview

This study presents the computational design of a
multi-epitope mRNA vaccine candidate against Marburg
virus using reverse vaccinology and immunoinformatics.

The research explores computational approaches for
identifying potential immune epitopes and evaluating
the structural and immunological properties of the
proposed vaccine construct.

Further experimental validation is required to
establish its safety and efficacy.
